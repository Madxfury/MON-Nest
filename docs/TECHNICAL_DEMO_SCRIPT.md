# MON-Nest technical demo — maximum 3 minutes

**Recording gate:** Record only after the contracts in `src/deployments/monadTestnet.json` are deployed to Monad Testnet and you have done one rehearsal of every step below on the public site. Use two browser tabs: a **renter** tab and an **owner** tab (the deployer wallet owns the 36 paid demo homes). Fund the renter wallet with a little testnet MON. **Before each signature, select that tab's account in MetaMask** — the site warns you if the selected account does not match. These homes are illustrative; nothing grants a real tenancy.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Open `mon-nest.vercel.app`; scroll from the hero to the catalog. | “Renting in India often costs a month’s rent to a broker and a deposit you may never see again. MON-Nest lists homes with verified property records and keeps your rent and deposit in escrow on Monad, not in a stranger’s account.” |
| 0:20–0:40 | Open a paid home (e.g. Sea Glass Residence). Point to the verified property record and the escrow terms. | “This home’s ownership is recorded in a property registry. Renting it means locking the first month’s rent and a refundable deposit in an escrow contract. Testnet amounts are one-thousandth of the listed price.” |
| 0:40–1:15 | Renter tab: connect an account (passkey or existing wallet), click **Lock rent + deposit in escrow**, approve both transactions, open the explorer link. | “I create the agreement and fund it. The escrow tracks this agreement’s money separately, so it can never pay out another renter’s deposit. The owner hasn’t received anything yet.” |
| 1:15–1:45 | Owner tab: Profile → **Your escrow rentals** → **Manage**. Show the agreement, then **Release this month’s rent**. | “The owner sees every agreement on their homes. Rent is released month by month, minus a 2% platform fee instead of a month’s broker commission.” |
| 1:45–2:15 | Owner tab: **Close lease & return deposit**. Renter tab: **Withdraw** the deposit. | “When the lease ends, the deposit goes back to the renter automatically. If they disagree, either side can open a dispute instead: the money freezes, and an odd-numbered jury of staked jurors votes in secret with commit–reveal. Jurors who don’t vote lose their stake to the ones who do.” |
| 2:15–2:35 | Open a free home (e.g. Fernlight Studio) and book it for 0 MON; show **Rented** in the other tab. | “Twelve homes also offer free trial stays through a shared booking ledger, so every visitor sees the same availability.” |
| 2:35–3:00 | Scroll to **What powers the experience**. | “Contracts run on Monad Testnet; Mera passkeys let renters sign without a seed phrase; Alchemy serves reads through our server; Envio handlers and a Chainlink CRE workflow are built and tested for indexing and automated rent release. Our track is Consumer Products & Payments: protected rent for people who never need to know it’s a blockchain.” |

**Before recording:** Do one full rehearsal on the public site and confirm every receipt on the explorer. If a step fails, do not narrate it as working. Describe Mera as an integrated option unless a real passkey ceremony has passed on this domain. Do not claim hosted Envio, deployed CRE, Aurora bridging, real tenancies, user traction, or awarded bounties.
